# Banco de frases para presentar resultados anuales

Frases en inglés agrupadas por sección. La nota en cursiva explica cuándo usarlas. Sustituye lo que está entre corchetes por tus datos.

## 1. Apertura

- "Good morning, everyone, and thank you for being here." — *Saludo estándar y cercano.*
- "Today I'd like to walk you through our results for [year]." — *Anuncia el propósito.*
- "I'll start with the highlights, then go through the numbers, and finish with our priorities for next year." — *Presenta la agenda.*
- "There will be time for questions at the end." — *Aclara cuándo se pregunta.*
- "It has been a [challenging / remarkable / transformative] year for [organization]." — *Marca el tono del año.*

## 2. Mensajes clave (highlights)

- "Let me start with three key messages." — *Introduce los puntos principales.*
- "First, … Second, … And finally, …" — *Ordena las ideas.*
- "The headline is that we [exceeded / met / fell short of] our targets." — *Resume el resultado global.*
- "This was our best year since [year]." — *Destaca un récord.*
- "We reached a significant milestone: [milestone]." — *Presenta un hito.*

## 3. Cifras: subidas y bajadas

- "Revenue rose by [12]% to [€4.2 million]." — *Subida con porcentaje y cifra final.*
- "Operating costs fell by [5]% compared to last year." — *Bajada comparada con el año anterior.*
- "Net profit increased from [€1.1 million] to [€1.5 million]." — *De una cifra a otra.*
- "Membership grew steadily throughout the year." — *Crecimiento constante.*
- "Donations declined slightly in the second half." — *Bajada leve.*
- "Sales remained stable at around [€3 million]." — *Sin cambios.*
- "Year-over-year, we saw an increase of [8]%." — *Comparación interanual.*
- "This represents a [15]% increase on [2025]." — *Otra forma de comparar.*
- "We saw a sharp rise in [online sales]." — *Subida fuerte.*
- "There was a significant drop in [costs] due to [reason]." — *Bajada fuerte con causa.*

## 4. Comparación con objetivos

- "We exceeded our target by [3] percentage points." — *Superar el objetivo.*
- "We came in slightly below our target of [€5 million]." — *Quedarse un poco por debajo.*
- "We are on track to meet our [three-year] goals." — *En camino de cumplir.*
- "We delivered [95]% of what we planned." — *Grado de cumplimiento.*

## 5. Retos y lecciones

- "Of course, not everything went according to plan." — *Introduce los retos con naturalidad.*
- "Our biggest challenge this year was [challenge]." — *Nombra el reto principal.*
- "This was mainly due to [market conditions / rising costs / delays]." — *Explica la causa.*
- "We have learned from this, and we have already taken action." — *Muestra respuesta.*
- "To address this, we have [measure]." — *Describe la medida.*

## 6. Agradecimientos

- "None of this would have been possible without our team." — *Reconoce al equipo.*
- "I'd like to thank our [partners / members / donors / clients] for their trust." — *Agradece a terceros.*
- "A special thanks to [name / team] for [contribution]." — *Agradecimiento concreto.*

## 7. Perspectivas para el próximo año

- "Looking ahead to [2027], our priorities are clear." — *Introduce el futuro.*
- "We will focus on three areas: [area 1], [area 2] and [area 3]." — *Prioridades.*
- "Our goal is to [grow revenue by 10%] by the end of next year." — *Objetivo concreto.*
- "We are cautiously optimistic about the year ahead." — *Optimismo prudente.*
- "We expect [moderate growth] despite [uncertainty]." — *Previsión con matiz.*

## 8. Transiciones

- "Let's move on to the financial results." — *Cambiar de sección.*
- "Turning now to [our challenges]…" — *Transición formal.*
- "As you can see on this slide, …" — *Referirse a la diapositiva.*
- "To put this into perspective, …" — *Dar contexto a una cifra.*
- "To sum up, …" — *Resumir.*

## 9. Gestión de preguntas

- "That's a great question, thank you." — *Ganar unos segundos.*
- "That's a fair question. Let me explain." — *Ante una pregunta incómoda.*
- "If I understand correctly, you're asking about [topic]." — *Confirmar la pregunta.*
- "I don't have that figure with me, but I'll follow up with you after the session." — *No sabes la respuesta.*
- "That's outside the scope of today's presentation, but I'm happy to discuss it later." — *Pregunta fuera de tema.*
- "Does that answer your question?" — *Cerrar la respuesta.*

## 10. Cierre

- "To conclude, [year] was a year of [growth / resilience / change]." — *Mensaje final.*
- "Thank you for your attention. I'm happy to take any questions." — *Cierre y paso a preguntas.*

## 11. Cómo leer cifras en voz alta

| Escrito | Se dice |
|---|---|
| €4.2M | four point two million euros |
| $850K | eight hundred and fifty thousand dollars |
| 12% | twelve percent |
| 0.5% | zero point five percent / half a percent |
| +3 pp | three percentage points |
| Q3 2026 | the third quarter of twenty twenty-six |
| 2026 | twenty twenty-six |
| 1,250 | one thousand, two hundred and fifty |
| FY2026 | fiscal year twenty twenty-six |

*Ojo: en inglés el punto es el separador decimal y la coma separa los miles (4.2 = cuatro coma dos).*
